HomeRoll is a self-hosted photo and video backup and gallery app. You run the server on your own hardware; this app keeps your phone's library continuously backed up to it.

Once set up, this app can be used as a photo and video backup solution directly from your mobile phone.

<b>Features:</b>

* Automatic background backup of photos and videos (selective albums supported)
* Browse a timeline of your library on any device
* Semantic search (CLIP), OCR text search, and facial recognition
* Albums, shared albums, partner sharing, and public share links
* Memories ("x years ago"), global map view, duplicate detection
* Multi-user support on a single server
* Upload assets from your local computer/server using the HomeRoll CLI
* Reverse geocoding from image exif data
* Show asset's location information on map (OpenStreetMap)

<b>Privacy first:</b> no telemetry, no phone-home, no ads. Your photos never leave hardware you control.
